TOEZLANDIA Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026
TOEZLANDIA is a Telegram Mini App where players can build kingdoms, complete quests, earn Footprints, compete on leaderboards and optionally connect a Solana wallet for seasonal reward eligibility.

Information We Collect

When you use TOEZLANDIA through Telegram, we may receive information provided by Telegram that is necessary to identify your game account, such as your Telegram user ID, username and other basic profile information made available to the Mini App.

We also store information related to your TOEZLANDIA account and gameplay, including game progress, resources, Kingdom Power, quests, streaks, Footprints, leaderboard information, achievements, records and other game activity.

Wallet Information

Connecting a Solana wallet is optional. If you choose to connect one, TOEZLANDIA may store your public wallet address and verification status so the wallet can be associated with your TOEZLANDIA account and used for applicable reward features.

TOEZLANDIA does not request or store your wallet seed phrase or private keys. Wallet verification uses a signed message to demonstrate control of the wallet and does not authorize a blockchain transaction.

How We Use Information

Information collected by TOEZLANDIA may be used to save and restore gameplay progress, operate game features, maintain leaderboards, prevent abuse, verify linked wallets, administer seasonal rewards, troubleshoot technical problems and improve the Mini App.

Seasonal Rewards

Some TOEZLANDIA seasons may include reward eligibility or distributions. When these features are active, account, gameplay and verified public wallet information may be used to determine eligibility and administer rewards according to the terms of that season.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate TOEZLANDIA, such as hosting, database and infrastructure providers.

Public blockchain information, including wallet addresses and transactions, is inherently visible on the applicable blockchain.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect information associated with TOEZLANDIA accounts. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

Wallet connection is optional. You may choose not to connect a wallet and may still access game features that do not require wallet verification.

Third-Party Services

TOEZLANDIA may interact with third-party services including Telegram, Solana wallet applications and blockchain infrastructure. Your use of those services may also be governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as TOEZLANDIA develops or its features change. The date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the TOEZLANDIA team through the official $TOEZ Telegram community.
